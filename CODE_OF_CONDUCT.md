# Code of conduct

PdfThumbnails is a small project that anyone may use, report problems to and contribute to. Its
issues, pull requests and discussions should be places where everyone can take part without
being harassed or put down, whoever they are.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, also with people who are new to Windows internals, C++ or open source.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons, and accept that a decision may go
  another way.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep other people's private information private, and your own too: don't post documents,
  screenshots of documents, or details about someone that they haven't made public.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, slurs, threats, and sexual language or images.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including following someone around the
  project or across GitHub after they asked you to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, and deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Reporting

- **Content on GitHub** (a comment, issue or pull request): use the **...** menu on it and choose
  **Report content**, then **Report abuse**. This reaches GitHub's own staff, who act under
  [GitHub's Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
- **To reach the maintainer** about a conduct problem, open an issue that only asks to be
  contacted, with no details; the maintainer will reply and find a private way to talk.
- **Security problems** go to the repository's **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**
  (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), never into a public issue.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock a conversation, close issues or pull
requests, and block someone from the project, as far as the problem calls for. A first, mild
problem usually gets a friendly word; repeated or serious ones get a block. Reports are treated
in confidence.

This code applies in the project's spaces on GitHub, and wherever someone speaks for the
project.
